16:T482,<h1>Maximum Product of Two Digits</h1>
<p>Given a positive integer <code>n</code>, return the maximum product that can be obtained by multiplying any two digits found within <code>n</code>. You are permitted to use the same digit twice if it appears more than once in the number.</p>
<h2>How the Solution Works</h2>
<p>To find the maximum product of any two digits in <code>n</code>:</p>
<ol>
<li>We extract the digits of <code>n</code> by continuously taking <code>n % 10</code> and dividing <code>n</code> by 10.</li>
<li>We keep track of the two largest digits (<code>max1</code> and <code>max2</code>) seen during the traversal.</li>
<li>The maximum product will simply be the product of these two largest digits.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Complexity</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Time Complexity:</strong> $O(\log_{10}(n))$ since we process each digit of <code>n</code> exactly once, which corresponds to the number of digits in <code>n</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Space Complexity:</strong> $O(1)$ auxiliary space as we only store the two largest digits.</li>
</ul>
